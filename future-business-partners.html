Let's Love Well. Live Local Together

Love Well. Live Local brings local communities and businesses together in a different way.  Encouraging  people to shop local, support their neighbors, and discover businesses in their community.

What is Love Well. Live Local?

Love Well. Live Local. is a community membership that connects members with local businesses offering exclusive discounts or benefits.  Members pay $29.95 every three months for access to participating business offers throughout Raleigh and surrounding communities.  Our goal is to encourage community support of locally owned business, in order to help strengthen the places we call home. 

Does it cost anything to become a participating business?

Nothing! Participation is completely free.

There is no sign-up fee, no monthly fee, and no requirement to purchase a membership for your business to participate.  Your contribution is the discount or benefit you choose to offer Love Well. Live Local. members.  And as a participating business, you’ll also receive one complimentary membership for as long as your business participates.

How Does It Work for My Business?

1. Choose an ongoing member benefit
Select a discount or benefit that works for your business and can be enjoyed by members on an ongoing basis. You choose your offer and any applicable restrictions.

2. Complete the business agreement
Tell us about your business and provide the information we need to add your listing.  A copy of your logo and a short 2-3 sentences about your business, so we can add you to our Website, Member Portal and Community Marketing.  

3. Welcome members
Your business will be listed on our website and in the member portal. When a member visits, they can verify their membership by showing the verification post in their portal or providing your business’s member verification code.

4. Stay connected
Enjoy being part of a community that encourages neighbors to support local businesses, and local businesses to support one another.  Networking and supporting other businesses is highly encouraged!

What kind of benefit should I offer?

We want Love Well. Live Local members to feel welcomed and valued each time they support a participating business. That’s why we encourage benefits members can use more than once, rather than an offer available only on their first visit.

But ultimately, you decide what makes sense for your business. Some examples:

  • A percentage or dollar amount off an eligible purchase or service
  • A complimentary add-on with an eligible purchase or service
  • A complimentary gift with purchase 

You may set reasonable restrictions, such as which products or services are eligible or whether the benefit can be combined with other offers.  We ask that your offer remain consistent for the six-month pilot period.  Not sure what to offer? We’d be happy to talk through ideas and help you find a benefit that works for your business.

What is included for my business if I decide to participate?

As a participating business, you’ll receive:

  • A listing on the Love Well. Live Local website and in the Member Portal
  • One complimentary membership while your business participates
  • An opportunity for your employees to receive $10 off their membership every three months with an employee discount code
  • Opportunities to connect with local residents and other participating businesses
  • Optional QR code signage and marketing materials to help members access their portal and introduce others to the membership

Who can participate?

We welcome a variety of local businesses, including those in health and wellness, fitness, retail, food and dining, professional services, and more.  If you’re a locally owned business that loves supporting your community, we’d love to connect with you!
