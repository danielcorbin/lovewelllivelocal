How it works

Join once. Save at every partner.

  1. Become a member

    Check out securely. The founding membership is $29.95 and renews every three months. That rate holds as long as you stay a member.

    Follow the business's instructions when you redeem. You may be asked to show your active membership.

Our story

Loving well begins with connection

Love Well Connections was created around a simple belief: loving well begins with connection. Our purpose is to strengthen existing relationships and foster meaningful new connections within our communities.

We believe thriving communities are built when neighbors support neighbors, people care for one another, and invest in the places they call home. When a community thrives, everyone has the opportunity to love and live well.

The Love Well. Live Local initiative makes that possible by loving well through shopping local. By partnering with small businesses and service providers, we connect members with exclusive discounts and savings. In return, each connection supports a local livelihood, a dream, and the community we share.

Membership details

Everything your membership includes

  • Exclusive discounts and benefits from participating local businesses
  • Secure access to the member portal with current businesses and offers
  • New ways to discover local services, products and experiences
  • Updates as new partners and benefits are added
  • The satisfaction of strengthening your local community
Good to know
  • You need an active membership to use benefits.
  • Each business sets its own offer and any restrictions.
  • Benefits can change, and businesses may be added or removed.
  • Offers are not combined with other discounts unless the business says so.
  • Please review each offer before you visit or buy. Love Well Connections does not provide or guarantee the products or services partners offer.
