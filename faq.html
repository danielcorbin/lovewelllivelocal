Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about membership and about becoming a participating business. For the complete details, see the Membership Terms and the Privacy Policy.

For members

What is Love Well. Live Local.?

Love Well. Live Local. is a community membership that connects members with local businesses offering exclusive discounts or benefits. Members pay $29.95 every three months for access to participating business offers throughout Raleigh and surrounding communities.

How much does membership cost?

A standard paid membership is $29.95 for each three-month term. If a valid discount code is applied, the discounted price is shown before you check out. The founding rate of $29.95 continues for the life of your uninterrupted membership. If you cancel, let the membership lapse or have an unresolved payment failure, a later re-enrollment will be at the price then offered.

Does my membership renew automatically?

Yes. A paid membership renews automatically every three months unless you cancel before your next renewal date. We send a written reminder by email at least 15 days before each quarterly renewal.

How do I join and get into the member portal?

Choose Become a member and complete the secure checkout. Then follow the login instructions to open your private member portal. If you are already a member, use Member sign in.

How do I find a business or a service?

Use the Partner Directory page. Search by name or service, then narrow the results by category, county and town. Full offer details and redemption steps are in your member portal.

What does “Comes to you” mean on a partner listing?

Some partners travel to you, for example for home visits or in-home services, instead of working only from an office. Look for the Comes to you label on a partner card, or use the Comes to you filter on the Partner Directory page.

How do I use a member benefit?

Visit the participating business and follow its redemption instructions. You may be asked to show your active membership, for example by showing the verification post in your portal or by providing the business’s member verification code.

Can I use an offer more than once?

Participating businesses are encouraged to offer benefits that members can use more than once, but each business sets its own offer and any restrictions. Please review each offer before you visit or buy.

Are savings guaranteed?

No. Membership does not guarantee that you will use or save any particular amount. Businesses may change, suspend or discontinue an offer, or leave the program, and offers are not combined with other discounts unless the business says so. Love Well Connections does not provide or guarantee the products or services partners offer.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel at any time before your next renewal date through your online account, if available, or by emailing lovewellconnections@gmail.com with the subject line “Membership Cancellation.” Include the member name and email address associated with the account. Cancellation stops future renewals, and you will ordinarily keep access through the end of the term you already paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Except where required by law or expressly stated in a written promotion, payments are nonrefundable, and we do not issue partial or prorated refunds for unused time or benefits. If you believe you were charged after a timely cancellation, contact us promptly so we can investigate.

Who can join, and can I share my membership?

You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into a contract. Membership is for the enrolled individual only and may not be transferred, shared or resold.

Do you store my payment card details?

No. Payment-card information is processed by Payhip, Stripe and their financial partners. Love Well Connections does not directly collect or store your complete payment-card number or card security code. Read the full Privacy Policy to learn how we handle your information.

For businesses

Does it cost anything for a business to participate?

No. Participation is completely free. There is no sign-up fee, no monthly fee and no requirement to purchase a membership. Your contribution is the discount or benefit you choose to offer members.

What does a participating business receive?
  • A listing on the Love Well. Live Local. website and in the Member Portal
  • One complimentary membership while your business participates
  • An opportunity for your employees to receive $10 off their membership every three months with an employee discount code
  • Opportunities to connect with local residents and other participating businesses
  • Optional QR code signage and marketing materials to help members access their portal and introduce others to the membership
How does it work for my business?
  1. Choose an ongoing member benefit that works for your business.
  2. Complete the business agreement, and share a copy of your logo and a short 2-3 sentences about your business.
  3. Welcome members. They verify their membership by showing the verification post in their portal or by providing your business’s member verification code.
  4. Stay connected with a community that encourages neighbors to support local businesses and local businesses to support one another.
What kind of benefit can I offer?

You decide what makes sense for your business, and we ask that the benefit be ongoing, something members can use again and again, rather than a one-time offer. Some examples are a percentage or dollar amount off an eligible purchase or service, a complimentary add-on with an eligible purchase or service, or a complimentary gift with purchase. You may set reasonable restrictions, and we are happy to talk through ideas if you are not sure what to offer.

Which businesses can participate?

We welcome a variety of locally owned businesses, including those in health and wellness, fitness, retail, food and dining, professional services and more.

How do employees get the membership discount?

An eligible employee of a participating business may receive $10 off the membership rate in effect when they enroll, using an employee discount code entered during account setup. The discounted price is shown at checkout and applies to renewals while the employee and the business remain eligible.

How do I become a participating business?

Fill out the partner form with your business information and the member benefit you would like to offer. We will follow up with the business agreement. You can also contact us, or learn more about being a local partner.
